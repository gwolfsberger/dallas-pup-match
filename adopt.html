The adoption process

Four steps, built to make sure the match is right for you and for the dog.

Apply online

Tell us about your home, schedule and any pets. It takes about 10 minutes, and our chat assistant can answer questions along the way.

Matching call

A volunteer calls within 48 hours to talk through your lifestyle and suggest two or three dogs that fit.

Meet & greet

Meet your matches at the foster home or our weekend adoption events. Bring the whole family, including your current dog.

Welcome home

Sign the adoption agreement, pay the fee and take your new best friend home with a starter kit and 30 days of free trainer support.

Adoption fees

Fees cover care the dog has already received, which would cost far more on your own.
